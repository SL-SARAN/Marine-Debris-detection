Common Performance Challenges
Acoustic Shadows: 
Seafloor features and steep rock formations cast shadows that can mimic or obscure small target clutter.
Low Signal-to-Clutter Ratio: Hard-to-detect targets embedded in sea grass or high-reverberation sediment reduce mean average precision (mAP) scores.
Viewpoint Sensitivity: Changing AUV altitude and beam divergence degrade resolution at outer swath ranges.
